Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 24, 2026

The Heena Live in Concert ("Event", "we", "our", or "us"), proudly presented by the 24/25 Applied Science Batch of the University of Vavuniya, is dedicated to upholding the privacy of all attendees, purchasers, and online participants. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, and safeguard your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details when you purchase tickets, order merchandise, or register for packages:

  • First Name & Last Name
  • Email address
  • Contact / WhatsApp number
  • Student ID number (where applicable)
  • Payment credentials (processed securely via encrypted gateways)
  • Any additional details submitted during ticket checkout, official merchandise purchases, or shuttle package bookings

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used exclusively to:

  • Process ticket purchases, official merchandise sales, and transport package reservations
  • Deliver event updates, queue management announcements, and schedule alerts
  • Provide customer support and verify online check-in wristbands
  • Improve attendee safety, venue security, and legal compliance

3. Payment Information Security

All financial transactions on this portal are handled through secure third-party payment gateways powered by Brick Pay. We do not store, view, or retain full credit card or bank debit details on our servers.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. However, necessary data may be shared with:

  • Our authorized payment processing provider: Brick Pay
  • Fulfillment service partners (only as strictly required for shuttle coordination or merchandise distribution)
  • Sri Lankan law enforcement authorities if mandated by law

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to defend your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is retained only for as long as needed to fulfill event services and comply with statutory obligations.
