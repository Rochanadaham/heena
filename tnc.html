Respectful conduct is expected towards fellow attendees, performers, and staff. Any harassment or disruptive behavior will result in immediate removal without refund.

11

The organizers reserve the right to adjust the event schedule, artist lineup, merchandise allocation, or venue arrangements if operational conditions require.

12

Wristbands & Add-ons Collection

Online ticket buyers can collect their free wristbands and package add-ons at the check-in desk on the day of the concert.
